Cookie Policy

Last updated: September 17, 2026

Short version: The Pausely iOS app does not use cookies. Our website uses only strictly necessary local storage for functionality — no advertising or tracking cookies.

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They are used for various purposes including keeping you logged in, remembering preferences, and analytics.

The Pausely iOS App

The Pausely iOS app does not use cookies. The app stores data using:

  • UserDefaults / AppStorage: Your in-app preferences (theme, currency, notification settings)
  • Keychain: Secure storage for authentication tokens
  • Supabase: Cloud database for your subscription data (see our Privacy Policy)

None of these constitute cookies under GDPR or ePrivacy Directive definitions.

Our Website (pausely.app)

Our website uses minimal local storage — no tracking pixels, no advertising networks, no Google Analytics.

Storage item Type Purpose Duration
pausely_waitlist_email localStorage Remembers your waitlist signup to prevent duplicate submissions Until browser data cleared

This single localStorage item is strictly necessary for form functionality and does not constitute tracking. No consent banner is required under the ePrivacy Directive for strictly necessary storage.

Third-Party Services on Our Website

Our website uses system fonts (no Google Fonts CDN calls) and connects to Supabase only when you submit the waitlist form. Supabase may set session cookies for API authentication — these are strictly functional and expire at session end.

Your Choices

You can clear all website local storage at any time through your browser settings. This will not affect your Pausely account or app data.

Changes to This Policy

If we introduce any non-essential cookies in the future, we will update this policy and display an appropriate consent notice.
