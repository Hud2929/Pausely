### 13. Custom URL

**Format:** `linkedin.com/in/[firstname]-[lastname]-[school/gradyear]`

**Examples:**
- `linkedin.com/in/alex-johnson-lpci`
- `linkedin.com/in/maria-chen-2026`
- `linkedin.com/in/jordan-toronto-student`

**How to set:**
1. Go to your profile → "Edit public profile & URL" (top right)
2. Click the pencil icon next to your URL
3. Type your custom URL
4. Save
