Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 23, 2026  ·  Effective: September 17, 2026

Our promise: You are our customer, not our product. We never sell your personal data. We collect only what we need to provide the service.

1. Who We Are

Pausely ("we", "our", "us") is a subscription management application for iOS. We are the data controller for personal data collected through this app and website.

Data Controller Contact:
Email: pausely@proton.me
Privacy enquiries: pausely@proton.me

2. Information We Collect

Information you provide

  • Account data: Email address, password (hashed — never stored in plain text)
  • Subscription data: Names, amounts, billing dates, categories you manually enter
  • Support communications: Messages you send to our support team

Information collected automatically

  • App usage data: Feature interactions, session duration, crash logs (only if you consent)
  • Device data: Device type, OS version, app version (for compatibility and security)

Screen Time data (optional)

With your explicit permission, we use Apple's Screen Time API to power the Smart Pause feature. This data is processed locally on your device. We do not transmit detailed app usage data to our servers.

Gmail integration (optional)

Your emails never leave your phone. Email content is fetched and processed entirely on your device. Only the extracted subscription details (service name, amount, billing date) are saved to your account.

If you choose to connect your Gmail account, Pausely uses Google's Gmail API with read-only access to detect subscription receipts automatically.

  • What we access: Email headers (From, Subject, Date) and body content to identify subscription receipts
  • On-device processing: All email content is fetched and processed on your device — it is never transmitted to Pausely's servers
  • What we save: Only the extracted subscription name, billing amount, and billing date — never the email content itself
  • Read-only: We cannot send, delete, or modify any of your emails
  • Disconnecting: Revoke access any time at Profile → Settings → Import from Gmail → Disconnect, or at myaccount.google.com/permissions

Pausely's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We do not use Gmail data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose other than subscription detection.

4. How We Use Your Information

  • Authenticate your account and maintain secure sessions
  • Store and organise your subscription data
  • Send renewal reminders and billing notifications
  • Improve app performance and fix bugs (only if analytics is enabled)
  • Respond to support requests

We do not use your data for advertising, sell it to data brokers, or use it for automated decision-making with legal effects.

5. Data Storage and Security

  • All data is encrypted in transit (TLS 1.3) and at rest (AES-256)
  • Primary storage: Supabase — SOC 2 Type II compliant cloud infrastructure
  • Row-level security (RLS) ensures you can only access your own data
  • We retain your data as long as your account is active
  • Upon account deletion, all personal data is deleted within 30 days

7. Your Rights

You have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access: Request a copy of all data we hold about you
  • Correction: Update or correct inaccurate data
  • Deletion ("Right to be Forgotten"): Request complete deletion of your account and all associated data
  • Portability: Receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format (JSON)
  • Restriction: Ask us to limit processing of your data
  • Objection: Object to processing based on legitimate interests
  • Withdraw consent: Withdraw analytics or marketing consent at any time in app Settings

To exercise any right: in the app via Profile → Settings → Privacy, or by email to pausely@proton.me. We respond within 30 days (72 hours for urgent GDPR requests).

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority (e.g. ICO in the UK, your national DPA in the EU).

8. Data Deletion

You can delete your account and all associated data:

  • In the app: Profile → Settings → Privacy → Delete Account
  • Web form: pausely.app/delete
  • Email: pausely@proton.me with subject "Delete My Account"

All personal data is permanently deleted within 30 days of a confirmed deletion request. Anonymised, aggregated analytics data (not linked to you) may be retained.

9. International Data Transfers

Your data may be processed outside your country of residence (including the United States). We ensure appropriate safeguards are in place via Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) and Data Processing Agreements with all sub-processors.

10. Children's Privacy

Pausely is a financial management tool intended for users aged 17 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 17. If you believe a minor has created an account, contact us at pausely@proton.me and we will delete the account immediately.

11. Push Notifications

With your permission, we send push notifications for upcoming subscription renewals, trial expirations, and price changes. You can disable notifications at any time in iOS Settings → Notifications → Pausely.

12. Cookies and Tracking

The Pausely iOS app does not use cookies. Our website uses only strictly necessary functionality (no tracking or advertising cookies). See our Cookie Policy for details.

13. Changes to This Policy

We will notify you of material changes via in-app notification or email at least 14 days before the change takes effect. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.
