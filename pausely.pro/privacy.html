Privacy Policy

Our promise: You are our customer, not our product. We never sell your personal data. We collect only what we need to provide the service.

1. Who We Are

Pausely ("we", "our", "us") is a subscription management application for iOS. We are the data controller for personal data collected through this app and website.

Data Controller Contact:
Email: pausely@proton.me
Privacy enquiries: pausely@proton.me

2. Information We Collect

Information you provide

  • Optional guest mode: You can use Pausely without creating an account. In guest mode, your subscription list, decision history, Savings Receipt and Pause Plan stay only on your device until you choose to back them up with an account.
  • Account data: If you create an account: your email address and password (stored hashed, never in plain text). With Sign in with Apple: the email address Apple shares with us (which can be Apple's private relay address) and Apple's identifier for your account. A name you give, or the one Apple shares, is kept only on your device.
  • Your list: For each subscription you add: its name, category, amount and currency, how often it charges, the date of its next charge, its status (for example active, in a free trial, or cancelled), when its free trial ends, how many days ahead you want to be reminded about it, and any optional notes, billing method or cost-split count you enter.
  • Decision history and Pause Plan: When you are signed in, the keep-or-cancel events behind your Savings Receipt and your Pause Plan schedule sync to your account so reinstalling or switching devices never loses them.
  • Cancellation difficulty ratings (optional): After cancelling a subscription, you may optionally rate how hard cancelling was (1–5). Submitted ratings are combined into anonymous per-service averages shown to help other users plan ahead.
  • Age confirmation: That you confirmed you are 16 or older, when, and which version of the app and of this policy you had at the time (see section 10)
  • Support communications: Messages you send to our support team

Information collected automatically

Nothing for analytics. Pausely has no analytics, advertising or crash-reporting software in it and keeps no record of what you do in the app. As with any internet service, the servers that store your list receive your device's IP address when the app connects to them, and the sign-in service keeps the technical records every sign-in service keeps (for example when you last signed in), for security.

Kept only on your device

Pausely keeps these on your iPhone or iPad and nowhere else: a local protected copy of your list for offline access, widgets and iPhone search (Spotlight), the hours of use you enter, the reminders scheduled for you, and the service logos it has shown you. Local data files are protected by iOS hardware data protection.

Import from screenshots (optional)

You can pick screenshots of Apple Subscriptions or recurring charges to add subscriptions faster. Screenshots are read in memory on your device using Apple's on-device text recognition (Vision) and are never saved or sent to our servers.

Hours of use (optional)

You can type in how long you used a subscription this month, so Pausely can show what an hour of it costs. Pausely does not read Screen Time. The hours you enter stay on your device.

Find in Email (optional)

Find in Email is not yet switched on in the App Store version of Pausely. This section describes how it works where it is.

Your emails never leave your phone. Email is fetched and read entirely on your device. Only the facts of each charge (service name, amount, date) are kept, on your device. A subscription reaches your account only when you add it to your list.

If you choose to connect your Gmail account, Pausely uses Google's Gmail API with read-only access to detect subscription receipts automatically.

  • What we access: Email headers (From, Subject, Date) and body content to identify subscription receipts
  • On-device processing: All email content is fetched and processed on your device — it is never transmitted to Pausely's servers
  • What we keep: Only the facts of each charge (service name, amount, currency and date), on your device — never the email itself. The address of each inbox you connect, and what lets Pausely read it (the access Google or Microsoft gives, or an app password you enter), are kept in this device's Keychain
  • Read-only: We cannot send, delete, or modify any of your emails
  • Disconnecting: In the app at Profile → Find in Email → Disconnect, or at myaccount.google.com/permissions

Pausely's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We do not use Gmail data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose other than subscription detection.

Other inboxes (Outlook, iCloud, Yahoo and others) are read the same way: read-only, and on your device. An app password you enter is kept in this device's Keychain and is sent only to your own email provider.

5. Data Storage and Security

  • Data is encrypted in transit (TLS) and at rest
  • Primary storage: Supabase — SOC 2 Type II compliant cloud infrastructure
  • Row-level security (RLS) ensures you can only access your own data
  • We retain your data as long as your account is active
  • What stays on your device (section 2) is protected by iOS data protection
  • Deleting your account in the app deletes your account, your list, your synced decision history, your Pause Plan, and your age confirmation from our servers right away, and everything Pausely kept on your device

Our website and its waitlist

pausely.pro is hosted by GitHub Pages, which receives your IP address when you visit. If you join the waitlist, we keep your email address, the page you joined from and when, so we can tell you when Pausely is available, and send you one welcome email. Your entry stays until you ask us to remove it: reply to that email or write to pausely@proton.me. Deleting a Pausely account does not remove a waitlist entry, because the two are kept apart; ask and we remove it.

7. Your Rights

You have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access: Request a copy of all data we hold about you
  • Correction: Update or correct inaccurate data
  • Deletion ("Right to be Forgotten"): Request complete deletion of your account and all associated data
  • Portability: Receive your list in a structured, machine-readable format (a CSV spreadsheet, or a JSON file with your list and its history; both from Profile → Export your list, which can also make a PDF to print)
  • Restriction: Ask us to limit processing of your data
  • Objection: Object to processing based on legitimate interests
  • Withdraw consent: Pausely sends no marketing email, and an account email only when you ask for a code. To leave the waitlist, reply to its email or write to us. Turn notifications off in iOS Settings

To exercise any right: in the app, correct your list by editing it, take a copy from Profile → Export your list, and delete your account from Profile → Privacy & security → Delete account; for anything else, by email to pausely@proton.me. We answer every request within 30 days.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority (e.g. ICO in the UK, your national DPA in the EU).

8. Data Deletion

You can delete your account and all associated data:

  • In the app: Profile → Privacy & security → Delete account (deletes your account and data immediately)
  • On the web: pausely.pro/delete (it opens an email to us, already filled in)
  • Email: pausely@proton.me with subject "Delete My Account"

Requests by email are completed within 30 days. Anonymous cancellation-difficulty ratings are detached from your account and kept only as aggregate service scores with no link to you. We keep a record that a deletion took place; for a deletion made in the app, it holds nothing that identifies you.

9. International Data Transfers

Your data may be processed outside your country of residence (including the United States). We ensure appropriate safeguards are in place via Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) and Data Processing Agreements with all sub-processors.

10. Children's Privacy

Pausely is intended for users aged 16 and over. When you create an account, we ask you to confirm your date of birth to check you meet this minimum age. We use it only to check eligibility and do not store it; with your account we keep a record that you confirmed you are 16 or older, when you confirmed it, and the policy and app version at the time. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 16. If you believe a minor has created an account, contact us at pausely@proton.me and we will delete the account immediately.

11. Push Notifications

With your permission, Pausely shows notifications before upcoming renewals, before free trials end, and for your Pause Plan. They are scheduled on your device; nothing is sent from our servers. You can choose which ones you get in the app (Profile → Notifications) or turn them off in iOS Settings → Notifications → Pausely.

12. Cookies and Tracking

The Pausely iOS app does not use cookies. Our website uses only strictly necessary functionality (no tracking or advertising cookies). See our Cookie Policy for details.
