Refund Policy

Last updated: September 17, 2026

TL;DR: Annual plans get a 14-day money-back guarantee. Monthly plans are non-refundable but you can cancel anytime. App Store purchases are refunded via Apple.

Monthly Subscriptions

Monthly subscriptions are non-refundable. You may cancel your subscription at any time to prevent future charges. You will retain Pro access until the end of the current billing period.

Annual Subscriptions — 14-Day Guarantee

We offer a 14-day money-back guarantee on annual subscriptions. If you're not satisfied for any reason, contact us within 14 days of your initial purchase for a full refund.

To request a refund: email pausely@proton.me with subject "Refund Request" and include your account email.

Approved refunds are processed within 5–10 business days to your original payment method.

Free Trial

The 7-day free trial is completely free — no charges during the trial period. If you cancel before the trial ends, you will not be charged. Cancellation must be made at least 24 hours before trial expiry.

App Store Purchases

If you subscribed through the Apple App Store, refunds are handled directly by Apple, not Pausely. To request a refund:

  • Visit reportaproblem.apple.com
  • Sign in with your Apple ID
  • Find your Pausely purchase and select "Request a Refund"

Exceptions and Special Cases

Refunds may also be granted at our discretion for:

  • Technical issues that prevented use of the service
  • Accidental duplicate purchases (reported within 48 hours)
  • Billing errors on our part

Refunds will not be granted for:

  • Change of mind after 14 days (annual) or at any point (monthly)
  • Failure to cancel before a renewal date
  • Accounts terminated for violating our Terms of Service

How to Request a Refund

Email: pausely@proton.me

Subject: Refund Request

Include: Your account email and reason for refund

Response time: Within 48 hours
